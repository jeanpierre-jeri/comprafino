# Catalog usefulness and coverage

`pnpm audit:catalog-coverage` and `pnpm audit:availability` report the complete bounded catalog using root `DATABASE_URL`, without retailer calls or writes. `-- --timings` on the coverage command adds current DB basket/detail measurements. External DB values are validated; the retained guard refuses truncation.

## Definitions and usefulness

Quantity quality is intrinsic evidence, independent of age/stock. Direct KG quotes can have strong unit-price evidence without a contained sale quantity. Public generic options require a positive ordinary open PEN state, active listing, availability not explicitly false, current observation, current normalization fingerprint/version and a trusted URL. Public exact rows additionally require trusted automatic/current-version canonical identity. Stored associations alone are not current purchase approval.

Generic shopping potential requires a supported profile, fresh public offer, strong contained quantity and the profile's measure. Need-specific compatibility, package rounding, overbuy and preferred-savings gates still apply. Strict/preferred exact potential requires a trusted current canonical UN sale package, independently of generic substitution support. Basket potential is the union, not the sum, of those sets. Search-only options can remain useful without safe generic fulfillment.

## Family audit

GOOD requires at least six fresh safe potential candidates across all three retailers. LIMITED requires at least two with weaker count/diversity. POOR has fewer than two in a supported family. UNSAFE means products exist without supported generic substitution policy; exact purchases remain independently possible. These are internal audit classifications, not public ranking or a numerical quality score.

Counts use complete family buckets, separate from the UI result limit. Milk is a lexical audit bucket, not an invented substitution taxonomy. Unsupported soap/cleaning/shampoo scope remains explicit. Supported product families and retailer category evidence remain in core and retailer source configuration.
